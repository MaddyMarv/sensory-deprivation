### [Sensory Deprivation](https://www.nexusmods.com/games/warhammer40kdarktide/mods/1201)

Challenge mod that blinds you and/or mutes all sound during missions. Toggle the blindness and deaf challenges separately or run both at once. Also includes a LIDAR scanner you can keybind to pulse your FOV or do a full 360° scan to navigate in the dark, with adjustable range and auto-pulse. Optional explosion flashes (seizure warning) and a personal speedrun timer are in there too.
